Cooperation on a grid

The spatial game of Nowak and May (1992), where cheating always pays better but cooperators survive anyway.

The rules

  1. Every square is a cooperator or a defector.
  2. Two cooperators who meet each score 1. A defector meeting a cooperator takes b and leaves the cooperator nothing. Two defectors both get nothing.
  3. Each square plays one round against the 8 squares touching it and against itself, 9 games in all, and adds up its points.
  4. Then every square, all at the same moment, copies whichever of those 9 squares scored highest. A tie leaves a square as it is.

Defecting always pays better in a single meeting, so a lone cooperator has no chance. Yet cooperators packed into a block feed each other faster than the defectors on the edge can eat them, and cooperation survives.
